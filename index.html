          💡 Why Style Flexing Matters

          Style Flexing is the ability to adjust your personal communication and behavioral style to match the comfort level and preferences of others. It does not mean changing who you are; it means expanding your range of behavior to eliminate stress, improve trust, and build more effective relationships.

          How to flex when interacting with Analytics:

            Trục Assertiveness (Quyết đoán)

            → To increase Assertiveness
            Tell more often • Ask less often
              ← To decrease Assertiveness
              Ask more often • Tell less often

                Trục Responsiveness (Cởi mở Cảm xúc)

                ↓ To increase Responsiveness
                Be willing to open yourself to others • Exert control less often
                  ↑ To decrease Responsiveness
                  Exert control more often • Express emotion and feelings less often
